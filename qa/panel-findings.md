## 5. Close the screen-reader defects in the live and interactive parts  (effort M)

Yara rated the markup fundamentals as best-in-class and then found the defects you only get to make once the hard parts are done: the wire is an aria-live region with no pause control (WCAG 2.2.2 Level A, no low-frequency exemption), the 404 announces the homepage title, the success state never receives focus, six links read as 'Enter world 01 / repo / repo' with arrow glyphs spoken, and the chip and AI badges carry their meaning only in title attributes. ### I18 — Live wire region has no pause, stop or hide control (WCAG 2.2.2)
- **Status:** confirmed · severity major · effort M · raised by 1 reader(s)
- **Page/section:** home / the wire, below the hero — components/wire/wire-live.tsx
- **Where in code:** `components/sections/wire.tsx`; `components/wire/wire-live.tsx`
- **Problem:** The commit feed is an aria-live="polite" list that the SSE handler prepends to, with no visible control to pause it, so an arriving commit interrupts whatever a screen-reader user is currently reading. WCAG 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide (Level A) has no low-frequency exemption. The TimeAgo children inside the same list also re-render on a 60s interval, so a label flipping from '2 min ago' to '3 min ago' fires another announcement.
- **Fix:** In components/wire/wire-live.tsx: (1) take the relative timestamp out of the announced content — wrap the `<TimeAgo>` span in `aria-hidden="true"` and give the row an absolute accessible date (e.g. `<span className="sr-only">{new Date(e.date).toLocaleString()}</span>`), or hoist the ticker out of the `<ol>` entirely; (2) add a persistent, visible toggle button in the wire header (components/sections/wire.tsx) that flips a `paused` state — when paused, call `es.close()` / skip opening the EventSource and render the `<ol>` with `aria-live="off"` — and persist the choice in localStorage so it survives navigation. Cheaper alternative if the feed is not meant to be announced at all: drop `aria-live` from the `<ol>` and put new-commit announcements in a separate visually-hidden `role="status"` node that the same toggle silences.
